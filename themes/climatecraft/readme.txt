A warm, authoritative Full Site Editing block theme for HVAC contractors, heating and cooling companies, heat pump installers, and indoor air quality specialists.

== Description ==

**ClimateCraft** is a modern WordPress Full Site Editing (FSE) block theme purpose-built for residential and commercial HVAC contractors, air conditioning repair services, furnace and boiler specialists, ductless mini-split installers, and indoor air quality (IAQ) professionals.

Instead of generic corporate templates, ClimateCraft gives heating and cooling businesses the exact visual layouts homeowners look for when their AC fails in July or their furnace stops in January:

* **18+ Purpose-Built HVAC Block Patterns:**
  * Split HVAC Hero with Upfront Diagnostic Pricing, SEER2 Highlights & Heat Pump Illustration
  * Credentials & Rebate Strip (NATE Certified Techs, EPA Section 608 Licensed, Manual J Sizing, 10-Year Parts & Labor Warranty)
  * 6-Card Heating, Cooling & IAQ Bento Grid (AC Repair, Gas Furnaces, Inverter Heat Pumps, Whole-Home Air Purification, Ductwork Sealing, Commercial RTUs)
  * Interactive Before & After Heat Pump & Sealed Ductwork Retrofit Slider (Keyboard & Touch Accessible)
  * SEER2 System Efficiency & Federal 25C Tax Credit Comparison Table (14.3 SEER2 vs. 17 SEER2 vs. 20+ SEER2 Variable-Speed)
  * 4-Step NATE Diagnostic & Airflow Balancing Timeline
  * NATE-Certified Comfort Advisor & Master Technician Cards
  * 3-Tier Annual Comfort Club Maintenance Membership Table
  * Printable Dashed Seasonal HVAC Tune-Up & System Rebate Cards
  * Homeowner Heating & Cooling Review Grid
  * Accessible Native HTML5 `<details>` HVAC & SEER2 FAQ Accordion
  * 24/7 No-Cool / No-Heat Emergency Dispatch & Seasonal Booking Section
  * 4 One-Click Full Page Layouts (Homepage, Heating & Cooling Services Directory, Contact & 24/7 Dispatch Page, and 24/7 Emergency HVAC Triage Page)
* **3 Global Style Variations:**
  * **Default (Thermal Navy & Flame):** Deep thermal navy (`#0A2540`), high-contrast heating flame (`#C2410C`), and crisp ice-white surfaces (`#F8FAFC`).
  * **Arctic Precision:** Cool commercial chiller palette with deep slate, arctic cyan accents, and clean mechanical surfaces.
  * **Furnace Warmth:** Cozy residential hydronic and heating comfort palette with warm sandstone, copper-flame accents, and deep charcoal ink.
* **6 Custom Block Style Variations:** One-click pills in the block inspector for `HVAC System Card`, `Interactive Lift Card`, `Seasonal Rebate Card`, `NATE Certified Checkmarks`, `Soft Thermal Pill`, and `HVAC FAQ Card`.

== Privacy ==

ClimateCraft is engineered with strict privacy-first defaults:
* **Zero External Fonts or CDNs:** All typography relies on fast, native OS font stacks defined in `theme.json`. No requests are made to Google Fonts or any third-party server.
* **Zero Tracking or Telemetry:** The theme contains no analytics scripts, external pixels, or remote API calls.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= How do I edit the emergency dispatch phone number, state HVAC license number, and seasonal hours? =
Go to **Appearance > Editor > Patterns > Header** (and **Footer**). All contact details inside the top emergency dispatch bar and footer are standard Gutenberg blocks that you can edit directly.

= How does the interactive Before & After heat pump and ductwork slider work? =
Insert the **Before & After Heat Pump & Ductwork Retrofit Showcase** pattern from the block inserter. Click either image inside the block editor to replace the placeholder SVG with your own before and after installation photos. On the front end, visitors can drag the handle with a mouse, swipe on touch screens, or use the Left/Right arrow keys.

= How do I switch between the Default, Arctic Precision, and Furnace Warmth color schemes? =
Open **Appearance > Editor > Styles** and choose your preferred global style variation with a single click.
