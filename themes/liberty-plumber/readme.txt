Full Site Editing block theme for residential and commercial plumbing, drain cleaning, and water heater service contractors.

== Description ==

**Liberty Plumber** is a Full Site Editing (FSE) WordPress Block Theme crafted by [NextRankHQ](https://nextrankhq.com) for residential and commercial plumbing contractors, hydro-jetting drain specialists, tankless water heater installers, and whole-home repiping companies.

Built for **WordPress 6.8 – 7.2+** and **PHP 8.3+**, Liberty Plumber pairs a high-contrast industrial palette (Deep Liberty Navy, Safety Copper, Cool Slate Canvas, and Emergency Dispatch Red) with system font stacks, zero external telemetry, and conversion-focused plumbing patterns.

### Key Highlights for Plumbing Contractors
* **24/7 Dispatch & Upfront Pricing Architecture:** Sticky header with emergency dispatch strip, upfront written pricing callouts, and clear service hours tables.
* **Interactive Before & After Project Slider:** Keyboard-, mouse-, and touch-accessible before/after comparison slider for whole-home copper repiping and condensing tankless water heater conversions.
* **Tank vs. Tankless Comparison & Membership Plans:** Built-in comparison table for standard atmospheric tank vs. condensing tankless water heaters, printable dashed-border service coupons, and 3-tier annual home plumbing maintenance plans.
* **3 Global Style Variations:** Switch effortlessly between *Liberty Navy & Copper* (Default), *Warm Copper & Brass*, and *Commercial Industrial Steel* inside the Site Editor.
* **100% Local Privacy & Zero External Requests:** Uses native system typography and self-contained vector illustrations with zero external CDN calls.
* **WCAG 2.2 AA Accessible:** Keyboard-navigable menus, native `<details>` FAQ accordions, visible focus rings, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support.

== Privacy ==

Liberty Plumber does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or telemetry. All typography and vector illustrations are bundled locally within the theme package, resulting in zero external HTTP requests to third-party servers.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= How do I load the full plumbing homepage layout? =
1. Create and publish a new Page (for example, titled "Home").
2. In the Page Settings sidebar, set the Template to **Full-Width Pattern Canvas** or **Page (No Title / Pattern Ready)**.
3. Open the Block Inserter (`+`), browse the **Patterns** tab under **Liberty Plumber: Full Page Layouts**, and insert **Full Plumbing Contractor Homepage Layout** (or simply activate the theme—`templates/front-page.html` renders the full homepage layout automatically).

= Does Liberty Plumber require any paid page builders? =
No. Every section, service card, comparison table, coupon, and FAQ accordion is built with native WordPress Core blocks (`theme.json` v3) so your site stays fast and easy to edit.
